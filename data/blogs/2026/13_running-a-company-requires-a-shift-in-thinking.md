title: Running A Company Requires A Shift In Thinking
---
Running a company requires a shift in thinking. I have been doing the same tasks at individual level: marketing, developing, dealing with clients, but, being in a company is not the same thing. Here are some funny realisations:

> CEO vs CTO

Initially, we set ourselves thus: 

CTO: Dominique Theodore 
CEO: Me

But, as we worked through, we realised i was more cut to be the CTO and Dominique, the CEO.

> Documents

Dealing with companies as an individual means you don't have to figure out contracts or documents. You just receive (, read) and sign them.

Having a company and dealing with people requires good paperasses, something Dominique has great experience with!

> Moving slowly

Sometimes, you need to plan things out and make you sure you are doing the right thing. This slows you a little.

> Owning products

When you just develop, you almost never own a product, except of course if you are in OpenSource. Having a company and owning a product means you have to think of adoption and marketing of the product.

> Strategic thinking

When you have a team and assets, you need to think of ideas, selecting or demoting them, pricing etc etc etc. Lots of thinking.

I decided to incorporate Fody Soft to sprinkle some challenges in my life ^^.
